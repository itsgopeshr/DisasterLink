⚙️ DisasterLink Hardware & Firmware Architecture
B.Tech CSE Engineering Clinics

DisasterLink is a low-cost water-level detection and emergency alert system built on the ESP32 microcontroller and HW-038 water sensor, with real-time USB telemetry and location-tagged flood warnings.

1. ESP32 + HW-038 Pinout Configuration

  • HW-038 Signal (S): Connected to GPIO 4 (D4) (12-Bit ADC Input, 0–4095)
  • HW-038 VCC (+): Connected to 3V3 pin on ESP32
  • HW-038 GND (-): Connected to common GND
  • Green Alert LED: Anode (+) via 220Ω–330Ω resistor to GPIO 5 (D5), Cathode (-) to GND
  • Serial Link: USB UART at 115200 baud streaming WET,value / DRY,value

2. Flood Detection & Location Logic

  • Threshold Evaluation: Any analog reading above THRESHOLD = 300 triggers the WET state.
  • Visual & Audible Alert: The LED on GPIO 5 blinks rapidly (every 150ms) and the laptop dashboard triggers an 880 Hz audio alarm.
  • Location Tagging: Automatically attaches live GPS coordinates, or logs "Permission denied" if browser location access is blocked.
🌊 Flood Emergency Safety Guidelines
  • Move immediately to higher ground when an automatic FLOOD WARNING is triggered.
  • Do not walk, swim, or drive through moving floodwater (6 inches of moving water can knock a person down).
  • Disconnect electrical appliances if water approaches indoor floor levels.
  • Use the DisasterLink SOS Portal to log your location and await rescue confirmation (ACK).
